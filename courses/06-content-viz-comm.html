Part 0

Why this session exists

Most of the value you create is invisible until you can show it. This session is the make-it-visible layer: the skills that turn a thought into a chart, a sketch, an image, a deck, or a clean piece of writing. Three tracks share it - viz (mind-mapping, dataviz, sketchnoting, visual-storytelling, ai-illustration), comm (communication, business-english, public-speaking, negotiation, facilitation, corporate-training, course-design), and biz (economics, finance, value-investing, marketing, personal-branding, solopreneur). The token thread running through all of it: the cheapest way to make something look great is to review it, not regenerate it - and a good image prompt is a few lines of text driving all the expensive pixels.

Part 1 · viz track - dataviz + the sketch family

Visual explainers on tap 7 min live

Two kinds of visual live here. dataviz handles the rigorous one - charts, dashboards, stat tiles - as one accessible system. The sketch family handles the expressive one - cheatsheets, decision-tree posters, style-driven illustrations for a gallery. They share a pipeline: an idea gets a style DNA, then becomes a published visual.

Livedataviz - one chart system, not one-off plots2 min▶

Reach for dataviz before you write the first line of any chart, in any medium - an HTML artifact, matplotlib, Recharts, an SVG. It gives you a form heuristic (which chart for which question), an accessible color formula with a validator, and specs for stat tiles and dashboards so everything reads as one system.

  • When to reach for it - any chart, dashboard, KPI row, or "make this data look good" moment.
  • Token effect - neutral to positive: it prevents the recolor-and-retry loop by getting encoding right the first time.
★ Try it nowBefore you build this chart, apply the dataviz rules: pick the chart form for the question I'm actually asking, choose an accessible palette, and tell me the one thing most people get wrong about this chart type.
Real world

A team shipped four dashboards where the same status meant a different color on each one. dataviz fixes that at the source: one fill per meaning, a legend, and colors that survive both light and dark mode - so the set reads as one product, not four.

LiveThe sketch family - five skills, one gallery3 min▶

The sketch skills turn a topic into an expressive visual and publish it. They divide the labor cleanly:

Self-studyConcept before style - the wow bar1 min read▶

Polished is not the same as wow. A visual earns its place when it says something words can't - a tension, a contrast, a "huh, I hadn't seen it that way." Nail the concept first; the style skin comes second. A beautiful render of a flat idea is still a flat idea.

Part 2 · viz track - ai-illustration + design supervision

Image + design, gated for taste 6 min live

Generating images is the expensive part; describing them is cheap. So the leverage is in the prompt, and in a supervisor that shapes the request. Then everything visual passes one gate - impeccable - which raises craft inside your locked brand and never overrides it. A gate reviews; it does not rebuild, which is why it stays token-lean.

Livevisual-image-prompt & midjourney-cc-skill ⚡2 min▶

These two write the request instead of hand-tuning the result:

  • visual-image-prompt - writes ready-to-run prompts for Nano Banana / GPT Image with model, size, and quality picks. Reach for it for blog covers, slide art, posters, product shots, storyboards.
  • midjourney-cc-skill - Midjourney-specific: constructs prompts, chooses between --sref / --oref / style codes, scores output, debugs why a prompt missed.
  • Token effect ⚡ - a prompt is a few lines of text that drives all the expensive generation. Getting the prompt right beats regenerating five times.
★ Try it nowWrite me a ready-to-run image prompt for [a slide hero / blog cover about ___]. Include the model, size, and quality you'd use, and one alternate direction if the first misses.
Livephoebe-design-partner - the design supervisor2 min▶

phoebe-design-partner is the front door for any design task - decks, dashboards, pages, posters, brand systems. It orchestrates the installed design skills and re-fits their output to an editorial-bold default with disciplined encoding and accessible color. You describe what you want to look good; it routes to the right specialist and holds the standard.

  • When to reach for it - "design this", "redesign this", "make it professional", or any screenshot/table you want to look good.
Real world

A founder pasted a raw capability table and asked for "something board-ready." The supervisor picked the layout archetype, applied one fill per meaning with a legend, and handed back a slide that read in five seconds - without the founder naming a single design skill.

Liveimpeccable - the anti-slop taste gate ⚡2 min▶

impeccable (~15k★) is the final gate on anything visual. Two rules define it and they are the whole point:

  • It raises craft within a locked brand - spacing, hierarchy, alignment, the squint test - and never overrides your palette or fonts.
  • It is a gate, not a rebuild ⚡ - it returns targeted fixes to what exists, so it reviews rather than regenerating. That is why it is token-lean even on a heavy page.
Gate, not painter If you want a different brand, that's a design decision you make - not something impeccable does behind your back. It makes your choices land better; it doesn't replace them.
Part 3 · comm track - decks + written polish

Decks + docs that land 5 min live

The last mile of communication is a deck or a document, on-brand and clean. These skills split by artifact: what you're starting from decides which one fires.

Livedeck skills - learn a brand once, generate on it2 min▶
  • deck-partner - feed it any company's template once; it learns the brand (palette, fonts, logos, layouts) into a locked profile, then turns your content + slide idea into an on-brand .pptx.
  • ct-deck-partner - the Centience-branded version, matched to the official template.
  • elite-powerpoint-designer - a strong generic deck builder when there's no brand to match.

When to reach for which: named brand you'll reuse → deck-partner; Centience → ct-deck-partner; one-off with no brand → elite-powerpoint-designer.

Liveproof & humanizer-zh - the writing gate2 min▶
  • proof - polishes a markdown draft: tightens structure, fixes flow, catches the small errors. It's the written equivalent of impeccable - a gate on prose.
  • humanizer-zh - strips the tells that make writing read as AI-generated: the hedging, the throat-clearing, the over-balanced "on one hand / on the other." Leaves your voice.
★ Try it nowRun this draft through a humanizing pass: remove the AI-writing tells - hedging, filler transitions, over-symmetry - and keep my actual voice and every fact. Show me what you cut.
Self-studyWhy "gate" beats "generator" for docs1 min read▶

Asking a model to rewrite a doc from scratch spends tokens on everything, including the parts that were already fine. A gate reads what you have and returns edits - far cheaper, and it keeps your voice instead of flattening it into a generic one. proof and humanizer-zh both work this way on purpose.

Demo 1 of 3

Write a ready-to-run image prompt ★ 6 min · follow along

Feel how a few lines of text drive all the expensive generation. This is the cheap-text-drives-pixels move, done rather than described.

★ visual-image-prompt triggerUsing visual-image-prompt, write a ready-to-run prompt for this: [a hero image / cover / poster for ___]. Give me the model, size, and quality to use, the full prompt text, and one alternate direction. Don't generate - just hand me the prompt I can paste.

Pick one image you actually need this week - a cover, a slide hero, a poster.

Run the trigger. Notice the prompt is a few compact lines, not a paragraph of hedging.

Paste it into your generator. If it misses, tweak the prompt - not the settings - and note how fast that loop is.

Save the prompt shape. It becomes your reusable template for that kind of image.

Why this is a token move The prompt is cheap text. Generation is the expensive step. Investing in the prompt means fewer regenerations - the same reason a good grill beats a rebuild.
Demo 2 of 3

Run impeccable as a gate ★ 7 min · build your own

Watch impeccable return targeted fixes to a page you already have - not a from-scratch rebuild. This is the difference between a gate and a generator, made visible.

Take an existing page or component you built - one that works but feels a little off.

Invoke impeccable on it. Note that it asks about your brand, then holds it fixed.

Read the output as a punch list: spacing here, hierarchy there, alignment there - each a targeted fix.

Confirm what it did not touch: your palette and fonts are unchanged. That's the gate working as designed.

Gate promptRun impeccable on this page as a review gate. Keep my palette and fonts exactly as they are. Return a punch list of targeted fixes - spacing, hierarchy, alignment, the squint test - not a redesign.
Real world

Every session in this course, including this one, passes through impeccable before it ships. It never changed the clay palette or the type - it caught the cramped SVG label and the heading that broke the scan. Review, not repaint.

Demo 3 of 3

Distill a topic to one page ★ 5 min · build your own

Use sketch-cheatsheet to turn something you know into a single design-forward page - the most compact, most shareable artifact in the whole session.

Pick a topic you could explain in a meeting - a metric, a framework, a workflow.

Invoke sketch-cheatsheet on it. It researches, then distills to one page wearing a style-DNA skin.

At the approval gate, cut anything that isn't essential. One page forces the priority call.

Export the PNG. Notice it's now a shareable asset - a post, a handout, a slide - from one compact source.

★ Try it nowBuild me a one-page cheatsheet on [topic]. Distill it to the few things that actually matter, wear a clean style, and give me one hero visual. Ask me before you finalize the layout.
Homework

Try it yourself - this week ◐ 30-60 min total

  • Write three image prompts with visual-image-prompt for things you actually need. Generate once each and note how few regenerations you needed.
  • Run impeccable on one existing page. Write down what it fixed and confirm your palette and fonts were untouched.
  • Build one sketch-cheatsheet on a topic you know. Share it and watch whether one page travels further than a paragraph.
  • Take one AI-sounding draft through humanizer-zh. List the three tells it removed so you can catch them yourself next time.
  • Leads: have your team each bring one before/after visual - raw vs gated. The delta from the taste gate is the training.
